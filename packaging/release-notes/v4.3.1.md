## What's new in 4.3.1

More room for the picture in the new design, and the things you asked for after 4.3.

### Fold what you do not need

- **Two small buttons in the title bar.** The one on the left folds the navigation to its icons. The one on
  the right hides the settings side: quick setup on the home page, the settings panel in Pro mode. The
  picture takes the room, and the same button brings the side back. Both are remembered.
- **Pro mode's categories fold.** In the *All* tab, *Quality*, *Frame Gen*, *Color*, *Audio* and *Display*
  each fold and unfold by their title, and stay the way you left them. A tab of its own always shows its
  category.

### The picture stays on the home page

A capture card connected from the home page now plays there, in the middle of the page, with quick setup
beside it. Before, connecting always jumped to Pro mode.

- Prefer Pro mode? *Quick setup → Connecting → Open Pro mode when a capture card connects*.
- With *Fullscreen on connect* switched on, connecting opens Pro mode in full screen, as it always did.
- Legacy is unchanged.

### Fixes

- **Optical flow: the choice no longer disappears.** *Frame generation motion source* (Zero motion / Optical
  flow) was not shown at all while NVIDIA VFG was the method, which looked as if the choice had been
  removed. NVIDIA VFG, AMD FRC and PushFlow work out motion themselves and do not use it. The row now stays
  on screen for every method; for those three it is greyed out and says why.
- **A folded quick setup section stays folded.** With *Reduce motion* on, folding a section left its rows
  on screen, switched off; on a slow frame a section could stay half open. A folded section now takes no
  room, however it is folded.

### Linux and macOS preview 0.1.1

Thanks to **ShyVortex** for these three fixes.

- **Connect when PushFPS opens** now connects. With a single capture card nothing was ever remembered, so
  nothing connected at start. The card is remembered when it connects, also by name, and a card that shows
  up a moment after the start is still connected.
- **Audio input and output are remembered.** They were forgotten when the app closed, and a changed device
  list could select the wrong one.
- **The interface always draws with its theme.** On newer Qt versions it sometimes started without its
  colours, sizes and fonts.
