## What's new in 4.4.5

Fixes from your reports, and Linux and macOS catch up.

### Windows

- **A translated subtitle stays long enough to read.** With live translation the translated line went the
  moment its original ended, so the faster the translation, the less time there was to read it. It now stays
  for a time you choose (*Live translation → Translation stays for*, 0 to 5 seconds, 1.5 unless you change
  it), or until the next translation is there. At 0 it goes with the original line.
- **PushFlow half precision can be tested from the interface.** The *Test* button only said to stop playback
  first, and a paused picture still counted as playing. With something playing it now stops it and starts
  the test by itself. A result that says half precision is not faster on your card reads as that.
- **AMD and Intel cards: a lighter FSR.** When the graphics card shows fewer generated frames than you asked
  for, *Check my setup* offers FSR's lighter motion estimate with one button, and Undo puts it back.
- **PS5 Remote Play.** The bitrate row says what to pick: on a wired network 30 Mbps or more is sharper.
- **Recordings.** The sound tracks in a recording are named *PushFPS sound* and *PushFPS microphone*.
- **Capture cards.** The AVerMedia Live Streamer Ultra HD (GC571) is on the list, reported working by a user.

### Linux and macOS (preview 0.2)

- **The new design.** The title bar, the named navigation and the status strip of the Windows build, round
  four pages: Home (sources, the picture, picture and sound, quick setup), Professional mode (the picture
  large, every setting in one panel with an *All* tab whose blocks fold), Minimal (the picture alone) and
  Settings, with the same themes and accent colours. Contributed by
  [ShyVortex](https://github.com/DosukaSOL/PushFPS/pull/18).
- **The picture keeps playing when you change page.** Leaving the player used to disconnect the capture
  card. Only *Disconnect* does now.
- **Crosshair Hold.** The same rule as on Windows: the crosshair is found in the middle of the screen, kept
  out of frame generation and laid back over every generated frame, so it no longer flickers or doubles when
  the camera moves. *Quick setup → Crosshair Hold*; the row says when a crosshair is held.
