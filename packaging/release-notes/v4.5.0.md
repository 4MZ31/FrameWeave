## What's new in 4.5.0

Subtitles that stay sharp, a much easier PS5 setup, and fixes from your reports.

### Frame generation

- **Subtitle Hold.** Subtitles no longer smear up and down when the picture moves fast, for example while
  driving. Like Crosshair Hold, the line of text at the bottom of the screen is kept out of frame
  generation and laid back over every generated frame. Switch it on under *Quick setup → Subtitle Hold*.
  It is off unless you turn it on.

### PS5 Remote Play

- **Get your Account ID inside PushFPS.** *Get Account ID* now opens Sony's own sign-in page. Sign in, paste
  the address your browser ends on and press *Fill in*: PushFPS fills in your Account ID. You no longer need
  a lookup website, and you never paste a session code anywhere.
- **Pairing takes what you type.** The 8-digit number can be typed the way the PS5 shows it, with the space.
  When something is missing, the message names the one field to fix and shows at the top of the window.
  Opening the window with nothing paired searches for your PS5 by itself.
- **The controller you press is the one used.** If Windows lists another controller, a wheel or a second pad
  first, pressing a button on your controller makes it the one PushFPS reads. A stream that plays with no
  controller on the PC says so.
- **Reconnecting after a drop.** When the stream breaks off, PushFPS now waits until the PS5 has let go of
  the old session before connecting again, so it no longer gives up with error 2004.
- **Fewer stutters at high bitrates,** with a larger network buffer for the stream.
- **Hardware decoding on newer AMD cards** (such as the RX 9070 XT) no longer stops after a few seconds.

### AMD cards

- **FreeSync is detected.** The *Variable refresh* switch now works with a FreeSync display on an AMD card,
  the same way it does with G-SYNC on NVIDIA. FreeSync is switched on and off in AMD Software.
- **FSR upscaling gets motion at the right size,** so it keeps more detail in motion.
- **AMD FRC** uses its stronger motion search on every card with its own graphics memory, so fast camera
  turns at 1080p and 1440p double less.

### Capture cards

- **Sharper colour edges** for YUY2 and NV12 / P010 capture.
- **MJPEG with a manual colour range.** Choosing *Full* or *Limited* no longer stops an MJPEG format from
  connecting.
- **60 → 30 original.** The plain every-second-frame selection of earlier versions is back as its own mode
  in *Duplicate frame removal*, next to *60 → 30*.

### Also

- **Volume above 100 % no longer crackles.** Loud moments are turned down smoothly instead of being cut off.
- **Recordings of an odd-sized window** are recorded instead of refused.
- **Xbox** with decoding on the processor uses the stream's real size.
- A warning in the live readout was shown in Chinese; it is translated now.
- Tooltips behind an open window no longer pop up over it, the network address box is ready to paste into,
  and the performance orb's glow is no longer cut off.
- Crash notes say a little more about where a crash happened, still without any personal information.
