## What's new in 4.5.4

### PS5 Remote Play

- **Long sessions stay on the graphics card.** In a long session with some lost frames, PushFPS could
  move PS5 decoding from the graphics card to the processor for good. The picture then fell behind and
  the stream could stall and end. Frames the decoder never shows no longer count against the session.
- **One damaged frame no longer switches decoding to the processor.** PushFPS now waits for the next
  keyframe on the graphics card, and switches only if that fails as well.

### Recording

- **The record window shows your Settings.** A bitrate, upload preset or size chosen in
  *Settings → Recording* now shows as the selected option in the record window, and is kept unless you pick
  something else there. The window also says that it uses the same settings.
- Choosing a bitrate or upload preset in Settings now always records at that rate, even if *Smaller files*
  was picked in the record window before.

### Window and controls

- **Maximise has its own icon.** The window's maximise and restore buttons no longer look like the full
  screen button, and the full screen button says that it shows the picture alone.
- **The playback bar fits.** On the picture-only page the bar's right side no longer gets cut off; a
  long preset name is shortened instead.

### Linux and macOS

- Console and game profiles, the live split comparison, the latency readout over the picture, and Check my
  setup.
