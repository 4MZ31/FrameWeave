## What's new in 3.2.4: longer comparison clips

**Comparison clips can be longer, or as long as you like.**

- **90 and 120 seconds** join the lengths there were (10, 15, 30 and 60).
- **Until I stop**: a switch under the length. The clip has no timer and runs until you stop it: click
  *Comparison clip* again (it reads *Stop comparison clip* while one records, in the quick setup, in
  Streaming mode and on the video page) or press the recording shortcut (F8 unless you changed it). The
  tile shows how long the clip has been running.
- Both work in all three layouts: split, side by side and vertical. The length you had chosen is kept for
  when you switch *Until I stop* off again. The same choices are under *Settings, Playback*.

A clip without a length grows like any recording, and comparison clips record every frame PushFPS shows, so
keep an eye on the disk for long ones.

**Long recordings on a nearly full disk.** Ordinary recordings never had a length limit: they run until you
stop them or the disk is full. When one finishes, PushFPS rewrites it as a standard MP4 (the kind every
editor opens), and that copy needs the recording's size in free space once more. A long recording on a
nearly full disk used to start that copy anyway, fill the disk and only then give up. PushFPS now checks
first: if there is no room, the recording is kept as it was written (it plays everywhere, but some editors
such as Vegas Pro refuse that form) and a message says so.

**How it was tested.** In CI on Windows without a graphics card: the build, the unit tests (the free-space
rule), the QML tests (the new lengths, a clip without a length started in each of the three layouts, the
running time, stopping from the tile and from Streaming mode) and the install test. **Not tested on real
hardware**: no clip was recorded with a capture card for this release, so the timer-free path has only been
exercised against the test bridge, not by recording a real clip for several minutes.

**Windows Defender note.** Defender may flag new PushFPS downloads as `Trojan:Win32/Tecabans.STV!cl`. This is a false positive from its cloud check on new, unsigned files, and it has been reported to Microsoft. Always check the zip against `SHA256SUMS.txt` on this page.
