## What's new in 4.5.1

Profiles that remember, settings that always save, and less waiting between your button and the screen.

### Profiles and settings

- **A profile keeps what you change.** Changes you make after choosing a game or console profile are now
  saved into that profile, so choosing it again after a restart or an update brings back your latest
  settings instead of the old ones.
- **Settings always save.** If the settings file could not be read (for example after a crash during an
  update), PushFPS stopped saving anything for good. Now the damaged file is set aside, the last good copy
  is used, and saving goes on. PushFPS tells you once when this happens.

### Latency

- **Steadier delay after menus and pauses.** Frame generation's timing now keeps what it has learned
  through a menu or a still picture, so the delay no longer jumps back up and slowly creeps down again
  every time you return to the game.
- **Check my setup finds what adds delay.** It now points out a picture shown in a window (full screen
  skips a refresh of waiting), low latency switched off, Frame Pacing Repair, and waiting for every refresh
  on a screen without G-SYNC or FreeSync. Each comes with a button and an Undo.
- **Fewer stutters with frame generation.** When a generated frame would not be ready in time, PushFPS
  skipped it and showed the real frame twice: a short freeze, then a jump. It now gives that frame the
  little extra time it needs instead.
- **A lighter window while you play.** The audio bars in Professional mode and the recording light kept
  redrawing the whole window at every refresh while a picture played, taking time from the picture. They
  no longer do.
- **Lowest latency acts at once.** Switched on while a picture plays, it now goes full screen right away
  instead of at the next connection.

### Also

- **A capture card that does not answer no longer freezes the device list.** PushFPS waits at most a few
  seconds for each card, lists it anyway, and moves on; choosing another card or refreshing the list always
  works.
- PushFPS starts lighter: windows you have not opened are no longer made at start.

### Linux and macOS (preview 0.3)

- **Upscale to my screen** with NVIDIA Image Scaling, to 2K, 4K, 5K or 6K, with a sharpness slider.
- **Professional mode's tabs** (Quality, Frame Gen, Colour, Audio, Display) and its live meters, as on
  Windows, and quick setup in the same sections as on Windows.
- **Subtitle Hold**, as on Windows: subtitles no longer smear when the picture moves fast.

Upscaling, the new tabs and the quick setup sections were contributed by
