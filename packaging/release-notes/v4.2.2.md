## What's new in 4.2.2

Two fixes.

- **AI picture enhancement can be switched on in Professional mode again.** 4.2.1 moved the picture settings
  from the Frame Gen tab to the Quality tab and left the enhancement's own on/off switch out there. Without
  an enhancement layer there was then no way to switch it on in Professional mode. The switch is back, at
  the top of the Picture card in the **Quality** tab.
- **The Frame Gen tab says where the picture settings went.** A row there opens the Quality tab, where the
  switch, the look, the fine-tuning, the wide-screen fill and the crop now stand together.

The home page and streaming mode were not affected.

- **Settings → About: the update buttons no longer overlap.** *Check*, *Install update* and *View update*
  stood on top of each other; they now stand side by side.
