## What's new in 4.2.1

Five changes that players asked for.

### Upscale to my screen

A capture that is smaller than your screen looks sharp in a small window and soft in full screen: the
window shrinks the picture in one case and only stretches it in the other. Quick setup has a new switch
under **Picture**, **Upscale to my screen**. With it on, the graphics card enlarges the picture to the
screen's resolution before it is shown, so edges and text stay sharp in full screen.

- It uses the upscaler you already chose in Professional mode. If your card cannot run that one, it takes
  one that it can: FSR, or NVIDIA Image Scaling.
- On AMD cards FSR is FSR 4.1 where AMD's driver offers it for the card (Radeon RX 9000 and RX 7000 with a
  current driver) and FSR 3.1 elsewhere. The row says which version is running.
- A screen up to 1440p gets a 1440p picture, a larger one 4K. A size you set yourself in Professional mode
  is kept.
- **Check my setup** now says when the picture is smaller than your screen and offers the switch, with an
  undo.

Upscaling is extra work for the graphics card. If the frame rate drops, switch it off again.

### The before/after line

- **It moves in full screen.** With the split comparison on, hold the left mouse button on the picture and
  drag the line. Until now that only worked on the picture inside Professional mode.
- **The sides can be swapped.** *Swap sides* puts the original on the right and the enhanced picture on the
  left. It is next to the comparison switch in quick setup and in *Professional → Display*.

### AI picture enhancement: colour above 100%

The colour strength runs from 0% (the game's own colours) over 100% (the enhancement's colours) to 200%.
Above 100% it used to double every colour change, which broke colours where the change was already large.
Now small changes are strengthened as asked and large ones taper off, so the slider can be moved up without
the picture falling apart. Up to 100% nothing has changed.

### Professional mode

- **A wider panel in full screen.** The panel over the full-screen picture can be made wider: drag its
  right edge. A double click on the edge puts it back, and the width is remembered.
- **The picture's settings in one place.** The look of AI picture enhancement, its size and its fine-tuning,
  the wide-screen fill and the crop now stand in the **Quality** tab, right above the enhancement's own
  card. The **Frame Gen** tab keeps frame generation, the HUD, latency and connecting.
