## What's new in 3.1.5: a sharper PushFlow, and the right version number

**PushFlow's Fast tier keeps its detail.** Before, Fast ran the network at half size and enlarged the finished picture, which softened fine texture and text. Now PushFlow takes the network's motion and blend mask, enlarges *those*, and builds every pixel from the full-resolution real frames. On a panning fine-texture test this scores 25 dB against 17 dB for the old way (the Quality model gets 31 dB). At full size nothing changes: rebuilding reproduces the network's own picture exactly.

**Less work at 3X and 4X.** A pair of real frames is packed once; the extra frames only change the time.

**Half precision, checked on your card.** PushFlow now ships half-precision models too (with the motion warps and the blend kept in full precision). At start-up it compares them with the full-precision model on your graphics card and only uses them when they match; otherwise it stays on full precision.

**See what PushFlow is doing.** Streaming mode shows the precision and the network's time per frame while PushFlow runs, and the log has a summary every 30 seconds.

**PushFlowBench.** `PushFlowBench.exe`, next to `PushFPS.exe`, measures PushFlow on your graphics card (pack, network, rebuild, and whole pairs at 2X/3X/4X) and saves it as JSON. Please share it in a GitHub issue: real results from NVIDIA, AMD and Intel cards are what PushFlow needs most now. How it works: `docs/PUSHFLOW.md`.

**Recording in video playback mode.** Video playback mode now has its own Record section with the same recorder as Professional mode: record and stop with a timer, comparison clips (split, side by side or vertical for TikTok, Shorts and Reels, with the same lengths), instant replay, game sound and microphone, and a shortcut to the recording settings. Comparison clips of a video are labelled *Original* instead of *Console*.

**Video sound in recordings.** Capturing a window or screen (a browser playing YouTube, a video app) used to record without the video's sound, because that sound plays in the other app. A new *Record PC audio* switch (video playback mode, and *Settings, Playback, Recording* for Professional mode) records all PC sound except PushFPS. It turns on by itself the first time you capture a window or screen. Comparison clips and frame generation of a video now switch on duplicate frame removal (Auto) when it was off: a captured video repeats each frame, and without it the generated frames sat between identical pictures, so both halves of a clip looked equally smooth.

**Optimise for my PC shows whether it's on.** The tile used to be green from the start, which looked like it was already on. It's now grey until Optimise for my PC has chosen and applied settings, green while you keep them, and grey again after Undo.

**Fixed: the version.** 3.1.3 and 3.1.4 were built with Veyra's version number (2.1.9) as their own, by a setting inherited from Veyra. So *Settings, About* showed 2.1.9, and the update check kept offering the release you had just installed. Thanks to the Reddit user who reported it. Now About shows *Version 3.1.5* and *Built on Veyra 2.1.9*, and the update check also reads the installer's version record. On 3.1.3 or 3.1.4, the one-click update to 3.1.5 works and ends the loop.

**How it was tested.** In CI on Windows without a graphics card: the build, the unit tests (including the update loop fix), the PushFlow models checked on the CPU, PushFlow's whole path on WARP (Windows' software renderer) including the full-resolution rebuild and the precision self-check, a PushFlowBench run, the QML tests and the install test. Not yet measured on real graphics cards: PushFlow's speed, and whether half precision passes on NVIDIA, AMD and Intel.

**Windows Defender note.** Defender may flag new PushFPS downloads as `Trojan:Win32/Tecabans.STV!cl`. This is a false positive from its cloud check on new, unsigned files, and it has been reported to Microsoft. Always check the zip against `SHA256SUMS.txt` on this page.
