## What's new in 4.3

A new design. Every mode, every setting and every tool is still there; what changed is where things stand
and how PushFPS looks. If you prefer the design you know, it is one click away.

### The new design

- **Everything in one window.** Navigation by name on the left (Home, Pro mode, Streaming mode, Video
  playback mode, Export, Minimal, Settings), a title bar that shows what is connected, and a status strip at
  the bottom with the capture format, the frame rate, the latency, the graphics card's load and the output.
- **The home page shows the picture.** Sources on the left, the picture in the middle with the tools under
  it, and quick setup on the right, so a setting changes while you look at what it does. No more switching
  pages to see the picture and the settings together.
- **Nothing is hidden.** Over quick setup stands an index: *Console and game*, *Frame generation*,
  *AI picture enhancement*, *Picture*, *HUD*, *Latency and sound*. One click brings that part to the top.
- **Professional mode has an *All* tab.** It lists the settings of every tab in one place, under their
  names, with AI picture enhancement first. The five tabs are still beside it.
- **Dark by default, and yours to change.** Light, black, three tinted backgrounds and high contrast, each
  with seven accent colours.
- **A new start-up animation** with the PushFPS logo, about two seconds, skipped with a click or any key.
- **Hover.** The edge of whatever the pointer is on lights up in your accent colour.

### Legacy: the design you know

The **palette** at the bottom of the navigation opens *Appearance*. *Design → Legacy* gives you the
interface of 4.2 exactly as it was, dock, Sunset City logo and all. The same panel has the themes, the
accent colours and the start-up switches. In Legacy the palette is at the bottom left of the home page, so
the way back is just as short. The switch is also in *Settings → Appearance*.

Both designs have exactly the same features, and changing the design changes no setting.

### Fixes

- **AI picture enhancement can be switched on in Professional mode again.** 4.2.1 left its on/off switch out
  of the Quality tab; without an enhancement layer there was then no way to switch it on there. The switch
  is back, and in the new design its card is the first thing in the list.
- **Settings → About: the update buttons no longer overlap.** *Check*, *Install update* and *View update*
  stood on top of each other; they now stand side by side.
