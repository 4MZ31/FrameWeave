## What's new in 3.1.2: streaming mode catches up with OBS

**Webcam, window and video sources.** Each scene can now add up to four sources on top of the game in recordings and streams:
- a **webcam**;
- another **window**, such as a browser with alerts or chat, or a music player;
- a looping **video file**.

Put each one in a corner or the middle, pick its size, and make it square, rounded or round. Cameras can be mirrored. Sources run while streaming mode is on screen or while a recording or stream is going, so your camera is not on while you just play. A source that the next scene also uses keeps running, so your camera doesn't blink when you switch scenes. Notes:
- a minimised window has no picture;
- video files play without their sound.

**Desktop audio and an extra input.** The audio mixer has two new strips, each with a fader, mute and level meter. Both are off until you switch them on, and they go into recordings, the instant replay and streams you start after that.
- **Desktop audio** takes everything Windows plays except PushFPS itself (Discord, music, a browser), so the game is never in the mix twice.
- **Extra input** takes a second microphone, a line-in or an audio interface.

**Scene transitions.** Switch scenes with a **Cut**, a **Fade** or a **Slow fade**. Sources fade in and out with the scene.

**Everything from 3.1.1** is in this build too: duplicate frames are always removed before generating, the corrected 120 Hz tip, and the setup guide with targets for every monitor and the capture card section. **PushFPS-Setup-Guide.txt** is attached to this release.

**Tested how.** In CI on Windows:
- the build and the unit tests, including the new tests for the source shapes and the extra-audio buffer;
- the GPU tests on WARP: sources placed, scaled and blended in SDR and HDR, faded in and out, and scene fades;
- the QML tests for sources, transitions and the new mixer strips;
- the install test, which opens every main page.

Not yet tested on real hardware with a webcam, desktop audio or a live stream: reports are welcome.

**Windows Defender note.** Defender may flag new PushFPS downloads as `Trojan:Win32/Tecabans.STV!cl`. This is a false positive from its cloud check on new, unsigned files, and it has been reported to Microsoft. Always check the zip against `SHA256SUMS.txt` on this page.
